Integrated Islamic care for older people

Many older people need help from several places at once. We link them to Islamic bodies, welfare agencies, health services and volunteers in Kedah, and show where those links break.

    What we aim to doJoin help around one older person

    We link agencies so one person gets the right help.

    The problem

    One older person may need several kinds of help. Each agency only handles its own part.

    Kedah already has the agencies and programmes. What's missing is the link between them, so needs slip through when a referral, a rule or short staffing gets in the way.

    Our plan

    Five steps to a tested model

    We map who does what, listen to older people, design a model with the agencies, then test it with experts and a small trial.
