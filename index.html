i-CareElder

Integrated Islamic Elderly Care Dashboard

Many older people need help from several places at once. This dashboard maps the Islamic bodies, welfare agencies, health services and volunteers in Kedah, matches each need to the right ones and gives a suggested support plan. Older people can also give back through sadaqah, waqf or their time.

    What we aim to doJoin help around one older person

    We link agencies so one person gets the right help.

    The problem

    One older person may need several kinds of help. Each agency only handles its own part.

    Kedah already has the agencies and programmes. What's missing is the link between them, so needs slip through when a referral, a rule or short staffing gets in the way.

    Our plan

    Five steps to a tested model

    We map who does what, listen to older people, design a model with the agencies, then test it with experts and a small trial.

    1. MapWho does what now
    2. ListenWhat older people need
    3. DesignA shared model
    4. TestExperts and a small trial
    5. ImproveReady for other states
    The teamSix researchers from UUM

    From Universiti Utara Malaysia: the College of Business, the College of Law, Government and International Studies, and the Department of Student Affairs. We have studied ageing in Malaysia for over ten years.

      We build on four earlier studies. Three of them covered 2,966 people in total.
